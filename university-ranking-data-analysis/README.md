# University Rankings Analysis — EDA

## Project Overview

This project explores a dataset of 1,000 universities ranked worldwide for the 2018–2019 academic year.

The analysis focuses on the geographic distribution of universities and the relationship between global rankings and academic and research-related indicators.

## Objectives

- Explore the structure and quality of the dataset
- Analyze the geographic distribution of universities
- Examine relationships between ranking indicators and World Rank
- Analyze citations and research influence
- Compare alumni employment rankings
- Identify key findings and limitations

## Dataset

The dataset contains 1,000 universities and 12 variables, including:

- World Rank
- Institution
- Location
- National Rank
- Quality of Education
- Alumni Employment
- Quality of Faculty
- Research Output
- Quality Publications
- Influence
- Citations
- Score

8. Key findings and limitations

## Key Findings

- University representation is concentrated in a relatively small number of countries.
- The United States has the largest representation in the dataset.
- Research-related indicators show visible associations with global ranking.
- Alumni employment performance is concentrated among a number of highly ranked institutions.

## Limitations

The dataset represents a single academic year and includes only the top 1,000 institutions. The analysis is exploratory and does not establish causal relationships between ranking indicators and university performance.
